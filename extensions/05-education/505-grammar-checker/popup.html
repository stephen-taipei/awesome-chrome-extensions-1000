Grammar Tips

Their/There/They're

Their = possessive, There = location, They're = they are

Your/You're

Your = possessive, You're = you are

Its/It's

Its = possessive, It's = it is or it has

Effect/Affect

Effect = noun (result), Affect = verb (influence)

Oxford Comma

Use before "and" in lists: "red, white, and blue"

Semicolons

Connect related independent clauses without a conjunction

Apostrophes

For possession (John's) and contractions (don't), not plurals

Active Voice

"The cat chased the mouse" beats "The mouse was chased"

Avoid Redundancy

Skip "very unique" or "past history"

Concise Writing

"Now" instead of "at this point in time"
