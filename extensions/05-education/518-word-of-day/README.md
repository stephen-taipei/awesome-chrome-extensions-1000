# Word of the Day

A Chrome extension to learn a new vocabulary word every day.

## Features

- **Daily Word**: New word each day (12+ word database)
- **Complete Information**: Definition, example, origin, phonetics
- **Audio Pronunciation**: Hear the word spoken
- **Save Words**: Build your vocabulary collection
- **Learning Streak**: Track consecutive days of learning
- **Word Archive**: Access previously saved words
- **Share Function**: Copy word info to clipboard

## Installation

1. Open Chrome and navigate to `chrome://extensions/`
2. Enable "Developer mode" in the top right corner
3. Click "Load unpacked" and select this extension's directory

## Usage

1. Click the extension icon to open the popup
2. View today's word with full details
3. Click the speaker to hear pronunciation
4. Star the word to save it
5. Track your learning streak
6. View saved words in the archive

## Word Information

Each word includes:
- **Phonetic transcription**: IPA pronunciation
- **Part of speech**: Noun, adjective, verb, etc.
- **Definition**: Clear meaning explanation
- **Example**: Usage in a sentence
- **Origin**: Etymology and history

## Featured Words

Includes advanced vocabulary like:
- Serendipity, Ephemeral, Ubiquitous
- Eloquent, Resilient, Pragmatic
- Mellifluous, Quintessential
- Sanguine, Ineffable, Perspicacious, Ethereal
