# Spin Match

Stop the spinning reels at the right moment to match symbols and win points.

## Features
- Three spinning reels with various symbols
- Click to spin, click again to stop
- Match symbols for points
- Special jackpot for triple matches

## How to Play
1. Click SPIN to start the reels
2. Click STOP to freeze the reels one by one
3. Match 2 symbols for 10 points
4. Match 3 symbols for 50+ points (diamonds and 7s worth more!)

## Installation
1. Open Chrome and go to `chrome://extensions/`
2. Enable "Developer mode"
3. Click "Load unpacked" and select this folder
