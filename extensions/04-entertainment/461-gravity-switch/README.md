# Gravity Switch

A gravity-flipping arcade game where you navigate through obstacles by reversing gravity.

## Features
- Click to flip gravity direction
- Avoid obstacles by timing your gravity switches
- High score saved locally
- Smooth gameplay with gradient effects

## How to Play
1. Click on the game canvas to flip gravity
2. Navigate through gaps in obstacles
3. Score points for each obstacle passed
4. Try to beat your high score!

## Installation
1. Open Chrome and go to `chrome://extensions/`
2. Enable "Developer mode"
3. Click "Load unpacked" and select this folder
